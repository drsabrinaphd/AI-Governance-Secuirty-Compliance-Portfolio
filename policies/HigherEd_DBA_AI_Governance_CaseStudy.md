## 1. Executive Summary & Policy Context
As generative AI reshapes graduate education, doctoral programs require transparent governance frameworks that preserve academic rigor, protect data privacy, and establish evidence of authentic student learning. Developed from Dr. Sabrina Lampley's primary course notes and executive faculty analysis, this case study modernizes the BUS 700 Orientation Seminar syllabus by integrating Belhaven University's official *Faculty Guidance for Responding to AI Detection Scores* protocol. The framework operates on a foundational guiding principle established in Dr. Lampley's notes: **AI detection tools (e.g., Turnitin) prompt faculty inquiry rather than serve as definitive proof of academic misconduct.**

## 2. Syllabus Modernization: BUS 700 Orientation Seminar
*Based on Dr. Sabrina Lampley's BUS 700 Course Notes & Syllabus Modernization Analysis*

| Assignment Component | Legacy Approach (Dr. Lampley's BUS 700 Notes) | Modernized Addendum (Dr. Lampley's Policy Model) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Literature Review & Search** | Manual database searches via Google Scholar. | **AI Search Optimization:** Permitted for query generation; requires primary source verification to prevent hallucinated citations. |
| **Research Question Ideation** | Manual drafting based on core texts. | **Socratic Prompting:** Permitted with mandatory submission of an **AI Use Statement** outlining tool contribution. |
| **Writing & Formatting** | Strict adherence to APA 7th Edition. | **Mechanical Assistance:** Grammar tools permitted; text generation requires explicit APA LLM reference citations. |
| **Research Ethics & Data Security** | CITI Human Subjects Training completion. | **FERPA Guardrails:** Strict prohibition on entering unreleased research, personal data, or human subject data into LLMs. |

## 3. Institutional AI Governance Benchmarking
*Comparative framework synthesized from Dr. Sabrina Lampley's multi-institutional policy research:*

* **Belhaven University (Inquiry-First Stewardship - Primary Focus):** Incorporates Belhaven's institutional guidelines emphasizing educational resolution, required disclosure statements, and evaluating evidence of learning rather than relying solely on automated detection scores.
* **St. Petersburg College (Applied Business AI):** Emphasizes practical tool integration, responsible deployment certificates, and strategic business risk management.
* **Eastern University (Workflow Automation):** Leverages Center for Teaching, Learning & Technology (CTLT) guidelines to balance automated workflows with academic integrity standards.
